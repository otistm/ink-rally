# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- First playable version. Three stages: Pinewood (gravel), Chalk Hills (tarmac with crests and a gravel section) and Frostmere (snow).
- One-thumb driving: hold anywhere to go, slide left or right to steer, pull down to brake. Arrow keys work on a computer.
- A co-driver calls every bend before you reach it, on a card at the top and out loud (can be turned off on the stages screen).
- The car slides on loose ground, kicks up dust, leaves tyre marks, flies off jumps and lifts over crests at speed.
- Crowds wave at the hairpins and jumps, and the chevron boards on tight bends can be knocked flying.
- Two split times per stage against your best, a gold, silver and bronze time to beat, and your best time is saved.
- "Back on the road" puts you back if you crash or get lost; the clock keeps running.
